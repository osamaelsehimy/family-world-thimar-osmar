# Family World Original Visual Asset Policy

**Owner:** OSMAR Ecosystems  
**Product:** Family World – Thimar OSMAR  
**Status:** V1 working standard

## Purpose
Every visual used in Family World should strengthen a recognizable visual identity that belongs to this product. The goal is original work, not a visual imitation of another game, studio, brand, character, or interface.

## Non-negotiable rules
1. Do not copy, trace, extract, or lightly modify artwork, characters, maps, boards, card faces, icons, animations, screenshots, UI layouts, names, or distinctive trade dress from another product.
2. Do not use assets from games such as Candy Crush, Gossip Harbor, or other commercial titles as production assets or templates. Genre conventions may inform mechanics at a high level; expression, art direction, characters, layouts, and content must be original.
3. Prefer custom-created vector/CSS/SVG artwork and commissioned or generated assets created specifically for Family World.
4. Do not assume an image found online is free to use. No stock image enters production without a documented license and permitted-use check.
5. Use fonts and icon libraries only under their applicable licenses; retain attribution or license notices when required.
6. Do not depict real people, including the founder or family members, without their explicit permission and an approved source image.

## Asset creation workflow
- Write a short creative brief: product area, audience age range, cultural context, intended emotional tone, target screen sizes, and accessibility needs.
- Create original concepts with a distinct silhouette, palette, composition, and visual language.
- Review for accidental similarity to recognizable third-party characters, game boards, card art, logos, or UI.
- Check legibility at mobile size, contrast, color-blind accessibility, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep editable source files and record creator, creation date, source prompt/brief, revision, and license/rights status.
- Export optimized formats (SVG where appropriate, WebP/PNG for raster artwork) and include useful alt text.
- Add approved assets to a version-controlled product asset directory; never hotlink third-party image URLs.

## Family World visual identity direction
- Warm, optimistic, family-safe, premium and clear rather than visually noisy.
- A coherent visual language across the home hub, 100 m² apartment planner, games, learning worlds, marketplace, and community spaces.
- Islamic values are expressed through respectful conduct, trust, fairness, mercy, stewardship, and beneficial content. Avoid decorative or inaccurate use of sacred text.
- Characters should be original, culturally respectful, age-appropriate, and consistent across Arabic, English, and Chinese experiences.
- Game boards and cards need recognizable rules, strong contrast, clear interaction states, and touch-friendly sizing.

## Current implementation note
The first World Hub uses original CSS-built illustration elements and a CSS floor-plan preview. It introduces no downloaded or third-party artwork. The floor plan is a concept preview, not a construction document.

## Release gate
An asset is not production-approved until its origin and rights status are recorded, visual review is complete, and its mobile/accessibility behavior is checked.
